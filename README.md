# Severity & Case Classification

## Objective
Classify adverse drug reactions based on their seriousness and expectedness, and evaluate their clinical impact using pharmacovigilance principles.

---

## Case 01: Amoxicillin

**Drug:** Amoxicillin  
**Adverse Reaction:** Skin rash and itching  

### Seriousness
**Non-Serious**

The patient experiences skin rash and itching without evidence of hospitalization, life-threatening condition, disability, or death.

### Expectedness
**Expected**

Skin rash and itching are recognized adverse reactions associated with amoxicillin.

### Classification
- Seriousness: **Non-Serious**
- Expectedness: **Expected**
- Clinical Impact: **Mild to Moderate**

--

## Case 02: Metformin

**Drug:** Metformin  
**Adverse Reaction:** Lactic acidosis  

### Seriousness
**Serious**

Lactic acidosis is a potentially life-threatening adverse reaction and requires urgent medical management.

### Expectedness
**Expected**

Lactic acidosis is a recognized, although rare, serious adverse reaction associated with metformin.

### Classification
- Seriousness: **Serious**
- Expectedness: **Expected**
- Clinical Impact: **Severe / Life-Threatening**
